# {{COMPANY}} Brand Context

## Company Identity

{{COMPANY}} is an incident management and on-call platform for mid-market B2B SaaS engineering organizations.

**Canonical truth**: "{{COMPANY}} works because the pages stop arriving. Engineers don't babysit alerts — they keep shipping."

**Mission**: Turn alert noise into clear ownership, fast response, and learning loops, so engineering teams spend their nights building instead of triaging.

## Core Voice Pillars

### Clear
Simple, direct, reader-first. Explain jargon once, then use it confidently. Never pad with filler or assume expertise the audience doesn't have.

### Authoritative
Grounded in expertise; confident without arrogance. Support claims with facts and credible data. Never overstate certainty or sound legalistic or defensive.

### Approachable
Human, inclusive, warm — the opposite of bureaucratic. Write as a smart, friendly colleague. Never robotic, corporate, or flippant.

**Tone metaphor**: Trusted expert partner — confident in facts, kind in delivery.

## Messaging Principle: Removal Over Capability

{{COMPANY}}'s messaging emphasizes what *disappears*, not what's *added*. The win is the page that never fires, the escalation that never needed a human router, the Sunday that stayed a Sunday. Lead with the outcome, not the mechanism.

**Corporate → {{COMPANY}}**
- ❌ "We are thrilled to announce our revolutionary AI-powered incident intelligence solution."
- ✅ "We've made it simpler to get the right page to the right engineer — and to send far fewer of them."

**Too casual → {{COMPANY}}**
- ❌ "Getting paged at 3am is a total nightmare, right?"
- ✅ "Most on-call pain is a routing problem. Here's how to fix the routing."

**Verbose → {{COMPANY}}**
- ❌ "In order to ensure the mitigation of potential availability risks, teams should consider…"
- ✅ "To keep response times short, get these three things right."

## The Cardinal Sin: Fear-Based Reliability

Never lead with fear, outage-shaming, or downtime-cost math. No "your next outage will cost you $300,000 a minute." No screenshots of angry customers. {{COMPANY}} closes loops — it doesn't open new ones. If content introduces unanswered questions, surfaces edge cases, or transfers anxiety to the reader, it violates {{COMPANY}}'s voice.

The reader is already worried about reliability. That is why they are reading. Our job is to make them more capable, not more afraid.

## Humor Scale

- **A — Dry & Understated**: clever phrasing, mild irony, no overt jokes
- **B — Lightly Playful**: puns or relatable asides, human analogies
- **C — Conversationally Funny**: gentle humor, emojis allowed, self-aware (social channels only)

**Never use**: sarcasm, self-deprecation, snark toward competitors, jokes at the expense of an engineer's bad night, humor that undercuts credibility.
